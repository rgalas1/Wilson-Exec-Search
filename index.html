2) Strategic Signals

  • Cielo: Demonstrates a targeted approach with specialized roles like "Senior Recruiter (Pharma R&D)", and transparent compensation for specific US-based roles, likely driven by compliance requirements in those geographies.
  • Hudson: Focus on "Business Partners" over pure recruiters, indicating a shift toward advisory-led recruitment models.
  • Pontoon: Cross-pollination with IT staffing. Hiring for "Product Manager (Workforce Technology)" shows they are building internal HR tech products rather than just using off-the-shelf software.
  • Sevenstep: Focus on foundational recruiting. Roles like "Sourcing Specialist" and "Recruitment Coordinator" position them as an incubator for volume sourcing talent.
  • PeopleScout: Emphasizing "High-Volume Recruiters," signaling that they are capturing the mass-hiring market and competing on volume output.
Summary: Your competitors are diverging. Cielo and Hudson are moving toward specialized/advisory models, Pontoon is building technology, and PeopleScout/Sevenstep are doubling down on high-volume execution.

6) Battle Cards

Cielo Battle Card

Employer Brand Pitch: Tech-enabled talent acquisition; you aren't just a recruiter, you're a digital hiring expert.

Strengths: Some comp transparency (e.g., $62k-$89k for Sr. Recruiter), remote flexibility.

Poachable Talent Pools: Client Services and Implementation specialists who are burned out on massive enterprise rollouts.

"Cielo is moving heavily into HR tech implementation. If you want to focus purely on strategic talent acquisition and deep client partnerships without the software integration headaches, Wilson HCG offers a purer RPO environment."

Hudson Battle Card

Employer Brand Pitch: Flexible, consultative recruitment with strong APAC/UK presence.

Strengths: Variety of models (Project RPO vs Enterprise), strong executive search arm.

Poachable Talent Pools: Recruiters stuck in high-churn "Recruiter On Demand" models who want stable, long-term enterprise client relationships.

"Project RPO can mean bouncing between clients every few months. At Wilson HCG, we build long-term, embedded relationships with our enterprise clients."

Pontoon Battle Card

Employer Brand Pitch: Global scale backed by the Adecco Group ecosystem.

Weaknesses: Bureaucracy of a massive conglomerate; less boutique/specialized feel.

Poachable Talent Pools: Program Managers and Coordinators looking for more agility.

"Pontoon has massive scale, but that often comes with red tape. Wilson HCG offers the global reach but with the agility and specialized focus of a dedicated talent solutions firm."

Sevenstep Battle Card

Employer Brand Pitch: The best place to learn and grow your sourcing career.

Strengths: Massive internal training (160+ courses), highly structured promotion paths.

Poachable Talent Pools: Senior sourcers who have hit a ceiling and want to move into strategic Account Management.

"Sevenstep is a great place to learn the basics, but if you're ready to graduate from pure sourcing to full-lifecycle strategic advisory, Wilson HCG is the next step up."

PeopleScout Battle Card

Employer Brand Pitch: Award-winning culture backed by proprietary Affinix technology.

Strengths: Strong diversity/inclusion messaging, established global footprint.

Poachable Talent Pools: Talent Strategists who feel constrained by having to push proprietary internal tech stacks.

"PeopleScout relies heavily on their Affinix platform. At Wilson HCG, we are tech-agnostic, meaning you get to advise clients on the best tools for them, not just the ones we own."

7) Executive Summary

Market Hiring Temperature

Based on live data extracted from actual career portals, the RPO industry is deeply segmented. While Sevenstep and PeopleScout compete for high-volume executors, Cielo and Hudson move upmarket toward specialized Business Partners and Pharma R&D recruiters.

Where the Industry is Investing

The battleground shifted from "who sources fastest" to "who integrates best." Competitors like Pontoon hire internal HR Tech Product Managers, while Cielo shows its hand on compensation ($62k-$89k for Senior Recruiters).

Top Actions to Take

1. Leverage the comp black box by offering immediate transparency.
2. Benchmark Pharma/R&D recruiter comp against Cielo.
3. Capture Sevenstep sourcers ready to step up into full-cycle strategic advisory.

Biggest Vulnerability

The heavy shift toward high-volume/transactional recruiting at PeopleScout and Sevenstep risks alienating seasoned recruiters, creating a massive poaching opportunity for Wilson HCG.
