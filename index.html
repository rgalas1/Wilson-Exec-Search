3) Strategic Signals & Org Structures

  • Cielo: Successfully merging the "delivery specialist" and "client commercial" tracks. They are pushing their LATAM-based Regional Directors into direct US-client-facing roles. They are the prime hunting ground for Archetype A (Commercial Nearshore Leaders).
  • Hudson: Leadership leans heavily into bespoke solution design. However, their directors often handle volatile, project-based RPO rather than stabilizing massive enterprise shared services.
  • Pontoon: Heavily matrixed. Their VP-level talent is shifting focus toward internal HR tech product management, creating burnout for operators who prefer pure team building and delivery over software deployment.
  • Sevenstep / PeopleScout: Both feature massive internal promotion structures for volume delivery. Their VPs are highly metric-driven and excel at managing large headcounts, but they may struggle to adapt to WilsonHCG's agile, non-corporate "builder" environment.

6) Executive Summary

Market Leadership Temperature

The RPO executive market forces leaders to choose between a delivery specialist track and a client commercial track. The WilsonHCG mandate requires a rare hybrid: scaling nearshore shared services *and* owning commercial, US-facing client relationships.

Where the Industry is Investing

Firms like Pontoon are pivoting their VPs into tech product management, while Cielo dominates the LATAM nearshore commercial space. With average US-based VP of TA salaries hovering around $211,000, LATAM-based commercial leaders ($110k-$120k) are highly attractive.

Top Actions to Take

2. Map Cielo's Regional Directors in LATAM for the hybrid commercial/delivery experience.
3. Pitch the "Tech-Agnostic Builder" to Sevenstep and PeopleScout leaders.
